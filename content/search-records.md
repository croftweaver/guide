| Page (all Google) | Date on page | What it says |
|---|---|---|

## How long until AI assistants find a new website {#how-long}

**Looked for:** any published measurement, with a stated method, of how long ChatGPT, Perplexity,
Claude, Google AI Overviews or Google AI Mode take to first crawl, first list, or first cite a new
website. We kept apart a brand-new website (a new domain) and a new page on a site that already
exists.
**When:** 29 October 2026, 13:29 to 13:44 UTC. 26 searches, about 25 pages opened.
**Found:**

| Who published it | What it measured | Kind of site | Method stated? |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/how-fast-do-ai-search-platforms-cite-new-content/), Dec 2025 | First citation, ChatGPT and AI Mode | 81 new pages on its own site | Yes (company blog) |
| [MaxAEO](https://maxaeo.ai/blog/time-to-citation-ai-search/), Jul 2026 | First citation, seven AI services | 740 new pages on 132 existing sites | Yes (company blog) |
| [Presenc AI](https://presenc.ai/research/how-fast-ai-crawlers-index-new-pages), Mar 2026 | First crawler visit | 300 new pages on its own site | Yes (company website) |
| [Bili AI](https://bili-ai-nextjsnl-p.netlify.app/blog/how-we-got-a-brand-new-business-recommended-by-ai-in-under-30-days), Mar 2026 | First mention, three AI services | One brand-new business (its client) | No: a case study |
| Two developers' server logs on dev.to ([one](https://dev.to/joseph_easerva/how-i-tracked-which-ai-bots-actually-crawl-my-site-33fg), [two](https://dev.to/tianninglab/google-indexed-2-of-our-9200-pages-chatgpt-answered-questions-about-3-of-them-the-same-day-10j2)) | Crawler visits | Brand-new domains | Logs only, a few days |

None of these says who paid for it; each of the four companies sells AI-visibility or SEO services. We found no

**Result:** for a new page on an existing site, the company measurements above exist. For a
brand-new website, we found no published measurement with a stated method of the time to first
citation, for any of the five services; only the single case study and the logs above.
