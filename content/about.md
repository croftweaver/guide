# About this guide

## Who publishes it

The Croftweaver Guide is published by [Croftweaver](https://croftweaver.com), an open-source
toolkit for building small-business websites with an AI assistant. The guide stands on its own:
everything in it applies whatever you build your site with. At the end of each self-test, one line
says that Croftweaver can make the fixes.

The guide carries no ads and no paid placements.

## Who writes it

**Daniel Melter**, in Munich, maintains Croftweaver. He co-founded M-Squad in 2001 and leads it
today, and has worked in product development for mobile and telecom for more than 20 years. He also
co-organizes the GenAI Wednesday community in Munich; building its website is where Croftweaver
began. More about him on [croftweaver.com/about](https://croftweaver.com/about).

Found a mistake, or have a question? Write to hello [at] croftweaver [dot] com. Every correction goes
into the list at the bottom of this page.

## How AI helped

An AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) did much of the research and wrote the first drafts. Here is
what that means in practice:

- AI research assistants opened every source page and copied the exact words; a second pass
  re-read each quote from the page itself, because AI summaries of pages were sometimes wrong.
- A script also compares the quotes it can read with those copies whenever a page changes. Shorter
  quotes, and the quotes on [What we searched](/search-records/), are checked by hand.
- A second AI (Codex, by OpenAI) reviewed the tone. Daniel read and approved every page.

## How we check what we say

- **Every claim names its source and links it.** "Google says …" means a page by Google says it.
  The date in the link is the date that page gives for itself.
- **Studies come with who did them and who paid.** We copy both from the study. When a study
  doesn't say, we write "who paid: not stated".
- **When we find no source, we say so,** with *we found no published …* and a link to a dated
  record of [what we searched](/search-records/).
- **Our own opinions say "we suggest".** Everything else on a page has a source.
- **Every page shows when we last checked it.** We plan to re-check every page's sources every
  three months.

## Our own experiment

We publish this guide as a new site, and record how long Google, Bing and AI assistants take to
find it, with our own actions on the same timeline. See [How long it takes](/how-long/).
